# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

This policy explains what Poptopia ("the game", "we", "us") collects when you play, why, and the choices you have. Poptopia is for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Contact: our community Discord server, https://discord.gg/FrUZVJXTCx

## What we collect

- **Username and password.** The password is never stored; we keep only a salted scrypt hash. Used for your account and login.
- **Recovery code.** If you create a password recovery code, we keep only a hash of it. Used to reset a forgotten password.
- **Game progress.** Level, experience, gems, inventory, worn items, position, achievements, daily tasks, friends list, worlds you own and what you build in them, items on the ground, world visit history, race times, and stats such as blocks broken and time played. Used to run the game and save your progress.
- **Chat messages.** World chat and private messages, with your username, the world, and the time. Used to deliver chat and to investigate reports and enforce the rules.
- **Reports and moderation records.** Reports you send (reason, optional text, world), and bans or mutes applied to accounts. Used for moderation and safety.
- **IP address and device ID.** The app generates a random device ID. We store it and your IP address when an account is created and each time an account logs in from a new device or address. Used for security: login rate limiting, limiting alt accounts, blocking VPN and hosting networks at sign-up, and recognising logins from new devices.
- **Discord details, only if you choose to link Discord.** We use Discord's OAuth sign-in, receive your Discord ID and display name, and discard Discord's access token immediately. Used for optional password reset and new-device verification.
- **Trade and market activity.** Listings, trades, and gem totals, including anonymous daily totals of gems earned and spent. Used to run trading and to watch the in-game economy for abuse and inflation.
- **Support tickets.** If you open a ticket on our Discord server (for example to appeal a ban), we keep what you send us for as long as needed to resolve it. Discord handles that conversation under its own privacy policy.
- **Server logs.** Security events, reports, and errors, which can include your username and IP address. Used for security and debugging.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or payment details. The game does not contain ads or third-party analytics, and we do not sell your data. The game has no real-money purchases.

## Who can see what

Other players can see your username, level, worn items, achievements you pin, your chat in the world you are in, and the worlds you own. Your IP address, device ID, and private messages are visible only to us and our moderators. Private messages are also visible to their recipient.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers. Data is stored on a server we operate. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of players. If you link Discord, Discord handles that sign-in under its own privacy policy.

## Security

Passwords are hashed and login attempts are rate limited. We cannot restore lost or hacked accounts, so use a unique password and keep your recovery code private. No system is perfectly secure.

## How long we keep things

- Account and game data are kept while your account exists.
- Accounts, worlds, and locks that stay inactive for 1 year may be ended, reset, or removed (see the Terms of Service), along with their data.
- You can delete your account yourself in the game (Settings, then Delete account). The account and its personal data are then erased right away, as listed on the [Delete your account](delete-account.html) page.
- Chat logs and reports are kept for moderation after the account is deleted, with your account removed: your name is replaced by "[deleted]" and they are no longer linked to you.
- Sign-up and device or IP records are kept to prevent ban evasion and alt abuse, and are deleted with the account unless the account is banned or muted.
- Database backups are taken automatically (hourly for the last 48 hours, then daily for 14 days). A deleted account can remain in older backups until they expire, up to about 14 days.

## Chat, safety, and moderation

Chat is logged and moderators can mute players and ban accounts. You can report a player in-game (wrench the player, then Report), and world owners can ban players from their worlds. Reports are reviewed by staff. Bans can be appealed with a support ticket on our Discord server.

## Children

Poptopia is for players aged 13 and over. If you are under the age of majority where you live, you need a parent or guardian's permission to play.

## Your choices and rights

You can delete your account and data yourself in the game at any time, or ask us on the community Discord server (we verify it is your account first): see [Delete your account](delete-account.html). You can also ask for a copy of your data or a correction on Discord. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK, or California) you may have additional rights, and we will honour them on request. You can unlink Discord from the game at any time.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continuing to play means you accept the update.
